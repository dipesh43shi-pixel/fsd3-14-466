ChatGPT is an AI-powered conversational assistant developed by OpenAI. It can understand natural language and help users with tasks such as answering questions, writing and editing text, learning concepts, brainstorming ideas, coding, summarizing information, and more. It works by analyzing the context of a conversation and generating a response based on patterns learned from large amounts of data. ChatGPT is designed to make interacting with technology more natural, allowing users to communicate through simple everyday language.
